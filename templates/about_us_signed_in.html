What is Tōī Market?

Tōī Market is a legally ambiguous website: this means no selling merch if you're going to name it as said merch. It's created for art and tech students at school to sell the things they make.

This includes artworks, stickers, 3D models, electronics, product designs, and pretty much anything else that has been created in Art or Digi.

Who is it for?

Tōī Market is only available to students and teachers. Products are sold under usernames, allowing creators to build their own little corner of the marketplace.

It's also for anyone interested in very, very local, occasionally questionable-quality products and, hopefully, a good laugh.

How does buying work?

There are no online payments. All payments are made in person.

If you're interested in buying something, you can send an offer to the seller. The seller can then look through their offers and choose the one they like the most.

Once an offer has been approved, the buyer and seller can organise a meet-up location using the school's map and complete the payment in person.

What does the website do?

  • Allows students to create accounts and log in.
  • Lets Art and Technology students list products they have made.
  • Provides a marketplace to browse available products.
  • Allows buyers to send offers to sellers.
  • Notifies buyers and sellers about offers and approvals.
  • Provides a map to help organise in-person meet-ups.

Basically...

Tōī Market is a place for creative students to sell their work, support other creators, and possibly discover something amazing.

Or something incredibly questionable.

Either way, someone made it.
